## 4. App Review Notes (Crucial Guidance for Apple Reviewers)

> **IMPORTANT**: Apple Reviewers test macOS utilities with strict scrutiny on system permissions and usability. Copy and paste the following comprehensive walkthrough into the **"Notes"** field under **App Review Information**:

```text
Dear Apple App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Genie!

Genie is an ambient developer studio, spatial workspace, and local AI agent engineered natively and exclusively for macOS Sonoma and Sequoia using Swift 6.4 and Metal.

Crafted by a dedicated Mac user with over 25 years of experience using, mastering, and engineering Apple platforms, Genie is built strictly from first principles using native AppKit, SwiftUI, Metal 3, and StoreKit 2, with 100% App Sandbox compliance and zero third-party telemetry.

### 🌟 Quick 2-Minute Reviewer Verification Script:

1. Launching & Menu Bar Status Item:
- Genie launches as a lightweight, high-performance menu-bar utility with an icon in the macOS menu bar (represented by 🧞‍♂️).
- Click the menu bar icon or press Option + Space (⌥ Space) to open the living glass cockpit.

2. Testing the 81-Screen Spatial Canvas Matrix:
- In the cockpit, select the "Spatial Canvas" station or press ⌘N.
- Navigate across the 9x9 continuous coordinate plane using trackpad swipe gestures or directional keys. All off-screen tiles utilize SwiftDOM frustum culling to guarantee 120 FPS Metal rendering under a 35MB resident memory footprint.

3. Testing the Unified Chat Studio & Native Developer Tools:
- Open the Unified Chat Studio (⌘T or click the Chat tab).
- Type "/tail recent" or click the yellow "/tail" shortcut pill in the magic bar. Genie will retrieve recently opened computer documents and system items using Apple's official NSDocumentController and Spotlight APIs.
- Type "/finder" to open the integrated Miller-columns Finder pane.
- Type "/editor" to open the live syntax-highlighted code editor.

4. Testing Local Vision & AI Intelligence (100% On-Device & Zero Telemetry):
- Genie connects to local Apple Silicon runtimes via Ollama or MLX, or works completely offline with built-in AST synthesis.
- Optional Cloud LLMs (Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI) are strictly user-configured via Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK). Zero intermediate telemetry or tracking occurs.

5. Testing OLED World Clock Pillows & Battery Telemetry:
- Click the "World Clock" tab or look at the living complication widgets to see specular rim lighting, sweeping analog hands, and real-time global timezone scrubbing.

6. Testing In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2 Sandbox):
- Open the Menu Bar Dropdown or Settings > Subscriptions / Expansion Store.
- You can freely test any of the 3 premium subscription tiers ($29.00/yr Annual Pass, $2.99/mo Monthly, and $49.00 Founder's 2-Year Pass) using an Apple Sandbox tester account. A "Restore Purchases" button is readily available.

- Accessibility: Used solely to snap developer windows (Terminal / Xcode) alongside the workspace if requested. The app runs completely without it.
- Screen Recording: Used solely if the user explicitly triggers an on-screen visual inspection. The app functions completely without it.
```
