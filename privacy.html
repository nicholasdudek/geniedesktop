Privacy Policy

Product: Genie - Desktop Workspace (com.nicholasdudek.genie)
Effective Date: September 3, 2026 • Developer: Nicholas M. Dudek
Core Commitment: Genie is built with a strict Zero-Telemetry, Privacy-First Architecture. Genie runs 100% locally on your Mac. It collects, logs, profiles, tracks, transmits, and monetizes zero user data.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Genie does NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • IP addresses, MAC addresses, hardware serial numbers, or UUID identifiers.
  • Keystrokes, mouse coordinates, screen recordings, or clipboard contents.
  • Application usage frequencies, browsing histories, or launch logs.
  • Diagnostic crash telemetry, performance logs, or behavioral analytics.

2. Local On-Device Execution

All functionality is strictly contained on your local Apple Silicon or Intel Mac:

  • Application Directory Indexing: Queries local directories (/Applications) in ephemeral system memory. Never transmitted off your device.
  • Icon Rendering: Cached in an in-memory NSCache. No visual assets leave your machine.
  • Preference Storage: Saved to your local sandboxed macOS container via UserDefaults.

3. macOS App Sandbox & Zero Network Access

Genie operates under Apple's strict macOS App Sandbox. The application omits network client entitlements entirely. By operating system design, macOS prohibits Genie from initiating outbound socket connections, making HTTP/HTTPS queries, or communicating with remote servers.

4. In-App Purchases

Expansion packs and purchases are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. The developer never receives, stores, or processes your credit card numbers or financial credentials.
