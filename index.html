Spatial Windowing Engine

Direct macOS WindowServer level injection. Toggles seamlessly between clean desktop wallpaper layer (.desktopIconWindow - 1) and an elevated 120 FPS interactive spatial canvas (.floating or .desktopIconWindow + 1) without stealing menu bar focus.

NSWindow.Level DesktopWindowManager.swift Always-On Matrix
🎯

Smart Retractor & Inset Avoidance

Invisible top-edge cursor hover detector (location.y <= 6) glides the canvas smoothly away without intrusive buttons. Includes an intelligent Dock inset protection engine with a 0.45s rest period to prevent accidental triggers.

onContinuousHover DockAndDesktopManager Zero Visual Clutter

11-Tab Studio Command Center

An expansive menu bar hub controlling all parameters: Apps Matrix, Desktop Grid, Living Themes, Formations, Mouse & Trails, Entities & Pets, Spatial Sounds, Wallpaper FX, Motion Dynamics, Status Bar Styles, and VIP Packs.
