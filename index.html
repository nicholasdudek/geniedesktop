28+ Living Battery Styles & Hardware Telemetry

Replace your static system battery icon with fluid living gauges. Genie connects directly to Apple Silicon power rails and the System Management Controller (SMC) to deliver millisecond-level telemetry right on your status bar.

  • ⚡️
    Real-Time Wattage & Velocity: Live readouts of active power draw in watts (e.g. 14.2W), battery cycle count, and precise discharge speed.
  • ✨
    5 Signature Gauge Families: Liquid Glass Capsule, Cyberpunk Digital HUD, VisionOS Pill, Vertical Energy Column, and Minimalist Monochrome.
  • 🔋
    State-Aware Transitions: Fluid green charging bolts on AC power, ember amber in Low Power Mode, and red pulse below 20%.
  • 🎛️
    Zero-Delay Hotkey Control: Open the Studio Hub dropdown instantaneously via global hotkey or top-edge hover without stealing active window focus.
